### Key decisions

- **Stepwise merges over single jump**: chose 16 incremental merges to keep the history bisectable. If a regression surfaces, `git bisect` can localise the offending tag merge in O(log n) steps.
- **Did not pull from `upstream/chatwoot` directly**: per user direction, fazer-ai is the integration point.
- **Did not touch the dormant Logto code**: kept as-is. (User clarified mid-session that Logto is the *primary* IdP, not dormant — its reconfiguration is queued as Phase 5a.)
- **Did not realign WhatsApp provider against `whatsapp-api.git`**: out of scope; queued as Phase 5b.
- **Did not rebrand to Propria Cloud**: out of scope; queued as Phase 5c.
- **Did not commit/restore the 894 ghost files**: stashed for safety.
- **Did not push `codi-v4.9.0-fazer-ai.13` yet**: distinct from the safe-state push, awaiting smoke-test confirmation in Docker before publishing the upgrade branch as authoritative.
